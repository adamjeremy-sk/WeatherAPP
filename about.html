1. Weather Forecasting

WeatherAPP provides privacy-focused weather forecasting using multiple meteorological models.

Current conditions, 36-hour hourly forecast, 7-day daily forecast, AQI, UV, visibility, humidity, wind, and more.

  • Environment Canada GEM
  • NOAA GFS
  • NOAA GEFS
  • ECMWF
  • CMA-GFS

2. Sun, Moon & Astronomy

Detailed solar and astronomical information for photographers, outdoor enthusiasts, and stargazers.

2.1 Solar Information

  • Sunrise and sunset
  • Golden hour
  • Blue hour
  • Solar azimuth
  • Daylight duration

2.2 Lunar Information

  • Moon phases
  • Lunar illumination

2.3 Advanced Astronomical Tools

  • True Dark Sky Window (astronomical darkness and lunar interference)
  • Night Visibility Forecast (ambient light potential)
  • Solunar Activity Forecast (major and minor activity periods)

3. Weather Alerts

  • Official Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC) alerts
  • Opt-in notifications
  • Per-city alert management
  • Built-in test notifications

4. Privacy

No ads. No GPS permission. No user accounts. No behavioural analytics.

4.1 Privacy Principles

  • No GPS or location permission required
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No behavioural tracking
  • No user accounts
  • No data selling

4.2 Local Storage

Saved locations, preferences, and cached forecasts are stored locally on your device.

5. Design & Customization

  • Eleven handcrafted themes
  • Nine boutique color palettes
  • Light and dark options
  • Metric and Imperial units
  • Flexible location tabs
  • Customizable drop-down menu organization and visibility
  • 7-Day forecast placement control (inline or menu)

6. Support WeatherAPP

WeatherAPP is supported through direct purchases and optional community donations. The application is committed to remaining ad-free and privacy-focused.

7. Credits

WeatherAPP utilizes weather, geocoding, and air-quality information provided through Open-Meteo.

Official alerts are provided by Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC).
