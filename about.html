1. Weather Forecasting

WeatherAPP provides privacy-focused weather forecasting using multiple meteorological models.

  • Environment Canada GEM
  • NOAA GFS
  • NOAA GEFS
  • ECMWF
  • CMA-GFS

Current conditions, forecasts, AQI, UV, visibility, humidity, wind, and more.

4. Privacy

No ads. No GPS permission. No user accounts. No behavioural analytics.

4.1 Privacy Principles

  • No GPS or location permission required
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No behavioural tracking
  • No user accounts
  • No data selling

4.2 Local Storage

Saved locations, preferences, and cached forecasts are stored locally on your device.
