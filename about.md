\# WeatherAPP v1.3.0



\## Private, Ad-Free Weather \& Astronomy



WeatherAPP is a privacy-focused weather and astronomy application designed for users who value transparency, local control, and accurate weather information.



No ads. No user accounts. No GPS permission required.



\---



\# Weather Forecasting



WeatherAPP provides weather forecasts using multiple meteorological models, allowing you to choose the forecast source that best matches your needs.



Supported forecast models include:



\- Environment Canada GEM

\- NOAA GFS

\- NOAA GEFS

\- ECMWF

\- CMA-GFS



WeatherAPP provides:



\- Current weather conditions

\- Multi-day weather forecasts

\- Air Quality Index (AQI)

\- UV Index

\- Visibility

\- Humidity

\- Wind speed and direction

\- Cloud cover information



\---



\# Sun, Moon \& Astronomy



WeatherAPP includes detailed solar and astronomical information for photographers, outdoor enthusiasts, stargazers, and anyone who plans around natural light.



Features include:



\- Sunrise and sunset times

\- Golden Hour calculations

\- Blue Hour calculations

\- Solar azimuth information

\- Daylight duration

\- Moon phases

\- Lunar illumination percentages

\- Meteor showers

\- Astronomical event calendar

\- Calendar export for upcoming events

\- Solar alarms and reminders



\---



\# Weather Alerts



WeatherAPP can display official weather alerts and provide optional notifications for monitored locations.



Features include:



\- Official Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC) alerts

\- Opt-in weather notifications

\- Per-city alert management

\- Built-in test notifications

\- Dismissible in-app alert banners



WeatherAPP is not a replacement for official emergency communications. Always follow instructions from local authorities during severe weather events.

\# Privacy



WeatherAPP is designed around user privacy.



\### Privacy Principles



\- No GPS permission required

\- No location tracking

\- No advertising SDKs

\- No behavioural analytics

\- No user accounts

\- No data selling



Locations are selected manually by the user and forecasts are cached locally to improve performance and reduce network usage.



\### Local Storage



WeatherAPP may store:



\- Saved locations

\- User preferences

\- Forecast cache data

\- Theme preferences

\- Notification settings



This information remains on your device.
