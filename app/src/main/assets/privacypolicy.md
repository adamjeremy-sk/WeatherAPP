# Privacy Policy for WeatherAPP

**EFFECTIVE DATE:** 24 September 2026  
**LAST REVISED:** 26 September 2026

## 1. INTRODUCTION

WeatherAPP ("the Application," "we," "us," or "our") is a privacy-first weather and astronomical platform available on the Google Play Store. This Privacy Policy sets forth the principles and practices governing the collection, use, storage, and protection of any information in connection with your use of the Application.

WeatherAPP is built upon a foundational commitment to user privacy. We have engineered the Application with a strict zero-GPS, zero-telemetry architecture. By downloading, installing, or using WeatherAPP, you acknowledge and consent to the practices described herein.

## 2. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

In direct contrast to conventional weather applications, WeatherAPP does not collect, process, store, or transmit the following categories of information:

- **Geolocation Data:** The Application does not request, access, or utilise GPS, network-based location, Wi-Fi triangulation, Bluetooth beacons, or any other device location services. All location selections are made exclusively and manually by the user.
- **Device Identifiers:** We do not collect Android Advertising IDs (AAID), IMEI numbers, serial numbers, MAC addresses, or any hardware or software identifiers.
- **Telemetry and Analytics Data:** No usage analytics, behavioural tracking, crash-reporting identifiers, or session telemetry are transmitted from the Application.
- **Personally Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not collect names, email addresses, telephone numbers, postal addresses, or any other personal identifiers through the Application.

## 3. INFORMATION PROCESSED LOCALLY

The Application processes the following data strictly on your device:

- **Manually Selected Locations:** Any city, region, or coordinates you elect to view forecasts for are stored locally within the Application's cache.
- **Cached Meteorological Data:** A 60-minute in-memory and persistent disk cache temporarily stores forecast data retrieved from upstream meteorological providers. Cached data is automatically purged upon expiry of the 60-minute window or upon user-initiated clearance.

No locally stored data is transmitted to our servers or to any third-party server operated by us.

## 4. THIRD-PARTY DATA SOURCES AND AI SERVICES

WeatherAPP retrieves forecast and air quality data from the following publicly accessible services and API providers:

### FORECAST MODELS:
- Environment Canada - Global Environmental Multiscale (GEM)
- National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) - Global Forecast System (GFS) and Global Ensemble Forecast System (GEFS)
- European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF) - IFS/EPS
- China Meteorological Administration (CMA) - CMA-GFS (GRAPES_GFS)

### UTILITY AND AIR QUALITY APIS:
- **Open-Meteo Geocoding API:** Utilised exclusively to resolve your manually typed city or region names into geographic coordinates.
- **Open-Meteo Air Quality API:** Utilised to retrieve local air quality, visibility, and ceiling metrics.

### DATA TRANSMISSION TO THIRD PARTIES:
When the Application queries the forecast, geo-coding, or air quality APIs, it transmits only the meteorological parameters or text strings necessary to retrieve the relevant data.

Under no circumstances are device identifiers, user tokens, IP addresses, or personally identifiable information transmitted to any of the aforementioned providers. These services are operated by their respective institutions or corporations and are subject to their own data-handling policies. WeatherAPP exercises no control over the data practices of these upstream providers.

## 5. ADVERTISING POLICY

WeatherAPP is 100% ad-free. The Application does not display banner advertisements, interstitial ads, or video pop-ups. We do not integrate any advertising SDKs, employ tracking cookies, or participate in any programme that serves targeted advertising. WeatherAPP is supported solely by direct user purchases and voluntary community donations.

## 6. DATA SHARING, SALE, AND DISCLOSURE

WeatherAPP adheres to an absolute prohibition on data commercialization:

- We do not sell, rent, lease, or transfer any user data to third parties.
- We do not share data with data brokers, marketing agencies, or advertising networks.
- The sole exception to this principle would arise in the event of a legally binding request from a competent governmental or judicial authority, to which we shall comply only to the extent strictly required by applicable law.

## 7. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

All cached forecast data and user-selected locations are stored locally on your device using standard Android persistent storage mechanisms. No user data is stored on remote servers operated or controlled by WeatherAPP. Upon removal of the Application, all locally cached data is permanently removed from your device.

## 8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

WeatherAPP does not knowingly collect, process, or store personal information from children under the age of thirteen (13). Given the Application's zero-collection architecture, no data of any kind is gathered from any user, irrespective of age.

## 9. USER RIGHTS

You are entitled to request the deletion of any locally cached data by clearing the Application cache via your device settings or by uninstalling the Application. You may revoke any permissions granted to the Application at any time through your Android device settings.

## 10. PERMISSIONS REQUESTED

WeatherAPP requests only the minimum Android permissions necessary for core functionality. The Application does not request `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`, `READ_PHONE_STATE`, `READ_CONTACTS`, `CAMERA`, or `RECORD_AUDIO`. The Application requires only `INTERNET` access to retrieve meteorological, air quality.
