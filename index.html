Privacy First

WeatherAPP Libre is designed around privacy and user control.

  • No ads
  • No user accounts
  • No GPS permission required
  • No behavioural analytics
  • No advertising SDKs
  • Locations selected manually by you

Forecast Models

Select your preferred forecast source:

  • Environment Canada GEM
  • NOAA GFS
  • NOAA GEFS
  • ECMWF
  • CMA-GFS

Astronomy & Natural Light Planning

  • Sunrise and sunset
  • Golden Hour and Blue Hour
  • Solar azimuth information
  • Moon phases and illumination
  • Meteor showers and astronomical events
  • Solar alarms and calendar export

Weather Alerts

Monitor official Environment and Climate Change Canada alerts for your selected locations and receive optional notifications directly on your device.
