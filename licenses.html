2. Weather & Air Quality Data

WeatherAPP Libre fetches meteorological and air quality data via the Open-Meteo API. Open-Meteo is an open-source, free-to-use weather API that requires no API keys and does not track users.

2.1 Meteorological Models

The application routes requests to specific global models based on user selection. The underlying data is provided by national meteorological agencies under their respective open-data policies:

  • GEM (Global Environmental Multiscale Model) — Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC).
  • GFS (Global Forecast System) — NOAA / National Weather Service (USA).
  • GEFS (Global Ensemble Forecast System) — NOAA / National Weather Service (USA).
  • ECMWF (IFS 0.25°) — European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts.
  • CMA-GFS (GRAPES) — China Meteorological Administration (CMA).

2.2 Air Quality & UV Data

Air Quality Index (AQI) and UV Index data are sourced via Open-Meteo's Air Quality API, which aggregates data from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) and NOAA.

3. Weather Alerts

Severe weather alerts are sourced directly from Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC) using the Common Alerting Protocol (CAP-CP).

  • Source: MSC GeoMet / Datamart (ECCC Open Data).
  • License: Open Government Licence – Canada.
  • Attribution: Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence – Canada.

WeatherAPP does not alter the safety instructions or severity classifications of official ECCC alerts.

4. Astronomy & Algorithms

To ensure user privacy and offline capability, WeatherAPP Libre does not use external astronomy APIs or third-party ephemeris libraries. All solar and lunar calculations are performed natively on-device.

  • Algorithms: Based on the mathematical formulas published in Astronomical Algorithms by Jean Meeus.
  • Delta T Polynomials: Uses the official polynomial expressions for Delta T ($\Delta$T) published by Fred Espenak and Jean Meeus (NASA GSFC) in the Five Millennium Canon of Solar Eclipses.
  • Implementation: Custom native Kotlin/JVM implementation using iterative topocentric root-finding for precise rise/set times and azimuths.
  • Time Scales: Calculations account for Terrestrial Time (TT), Delta T ($\Delta$T), and atmospheric refraction.

5. Geocoding & Location

Location search and geocoding (converting city names to coordinates) is handled via the Open-Meteo Geocoding API.

  • Source: Open-Meteo Geocoding API (sourcing from GeoNames and other open geographic databases).
  • License: GeoNames data is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License.
