Privacy at a glance: No account. No GPS permission. No ads. No developer-operated analytics. Manually selected locations are sent to Open-Meteo to provide requested information. 1. Introduction

WeatherAPP Libre ("WeatherAPP," "the Application," "we," "us," or "our") provides weather, air-quality, solar, lunar, and astronomical information while minimizing the information processed about its users.

WeatherAPP does not require an account, does not access Android device-location services, does not contain advertising SDKs, and does not include developer-operated analytics, behavioural tracking, or crash-reporting services.

To provide forecasts and location-search results, WeatherAPP sends manually entered search terms and selected geographic coordinates to third-party weather services. This Privacy Policy explains what is processed locally, what is transmitted to third parties, which permissions may be requested, and the choices available to users.

By installing or using WeatherAPP, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information WeatherAPP Does Not Collect

WeatherAPP does not intentionally collect or maintain:

  • Device location from Android services: WeatherAPP does not request or access precise or approximate device location through GPS, cellular positioning, Wi-Fi positioning, Bluetooth beacons, or Android location services.
  • Advertising identifiers: WeatherAPP does not collect the Android Advertising ID or integrate advertising SDKs.
  • Persistent hardware identifiers: WeatherAPP does not intentionally collect IMEI numbers, device serial numbers, MAC addresses, or similar identifiers.
  • Account information: WeatherAPP does not require an account and does not collect usernames or passwords.
  • Contact information: WeatherAPP does not collect names, telephone numbers, postal addresses, or email addresses through normal use.
  • Developer-operated analytics: WeatherAPP does not include developer-operated usage analytics, behavioural profiling, session tracking, or advertising measurement.
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data: WeatherAPP does not request access to contacts, photos, the microphone, or the camera.

3. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

WeatherAPP may store the following information locally in Android application storage.

3.1 Manually Selected Locations

Locations you search for, select, save, or assign to location tabs may be stored locally. This may include a place name, administrative region, country, latitude, and longitude.

3.2 Preferences

WeatherAPP may store theme and display settings, units, selected forecast model, saved location tabs, default and recent locations, cache settings, notification preferences, solar-alarm lead time, dismissed-advisory state, and other interface preferences required to preserve Application behaviour.

3.3 Cached Weather Data

Weather forecast responses may be stored locally in memory and persistent Android application storage. The cache lifetime may be configurable and determines when cached information is considered expired and should no longer be treated as current.

Expired entries may remain until replaced, cleared through Android settings or an in-app clearing function, or removed when the Application is uninstalled.

3.4 Astronomical Information

Some lunar and astronomical information is bundled with the Application or calculated locally. This bundled or calculated information is not transmitted to WeatherAPP-operated servers.

WeatherAPP does not operate a developer-controlled server for user accounts, analytics profiles, location histories, or forecast caching.

4. Information Transmitted to Third-Party Services

WeatherAPP requires internet access to provide weather, air-quality, geocoding, and update-related functionality.

4.1 Location Search

When you type a city, region, or place name into location search, the search text is transmitted to the Open-Meteo Geocoding API so matching places and coordinates can be returned.

4.2 Weather and Air-Quality Requests

When you select or view a location, WeatherAPP may transmit its latitude and longitude, the requested forecast model, requested variables, forecast period, time-zone parameters, and other technical parameters required to return requested weather or air-quality information.

WeatherAPP does not intentionally add advertising identifiers, account identifiers, contact information, or persistent device identifiers to these requests.

As with any internet service, providers may automatically receive standard network and request information, such as the requesting IP address, request date and time, requested URL, and connection metadata. WeatherAPP does not control their independent logging, security, or retention practices.

5. Weather and Location Data Providers

WeatherAPP retrieves weather, geocoding, and air-quality information through Open-Meteo.

Open-Meteo processes and normalizes data from meteorological organizations and forecast systems that may include Environment and Climate Change Canada or the Canadian Meteorological Centre, NOAA, ECMWF, the China Meteorological Administration, and other providers selected by Open-Meteo's best-match service.

These organizations provide or operate forecast models. WeatherAPP does not necessarily connect directly to them.

  • Open-Meteo: https://open-meteo.com/
  • Open-Meteo privacy information: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms#privacy
  • Open-Meteo licence: https://open-meteo.com/en/licence

5.1 Air Quality, Visibility, and Estimated Cloud Base

The Open-Meteo Air Quality API is used to retrieve supported air-quality information, such as the current US Air Quality Index. Visibility is retrieved through the weather forecast service when available.

Any value labelled Estimated cloud base is calculated locally from available weather information. It is an estimate and is not an official aviation ceiling observation.

6. Official Alerts and Forecast Advisories

WeatherAPP may display official weather alerts only when they are obtained from an identified official alert source.

WeatherAPP may also display model-derived forecast advisories. A model-derived advisory is not an official government warning and will be identified separately from official alerts.

Users should follow local authorities and official emergency-alert services during hazardous weather. WeatherAPP is not a substitute for official emergency communications.

7. Google Play and Application Updates

WeatherAPP may interact with Google Play services to determine whether an update is available or to open its store listing. Google may process information under its own policies and account settings.

WeatherAPP does not receive your Google account password or payment-card information. Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms and policies.

8. User-Initiated External Actions

WeatherAPP may let you open a donation page, open its Google Play listing, create an alarm through a clock application, or create an event through a calendar application.

These actions occur only after you select the relevant option. Receiving websites and applications may process information under their own privacy policies. WeatherAPP does not control those services.

9. Advertising and Analytics

WeatherAPP is ad-free. It does not display advertisements, integrate advertising SDKs, use targeted-advertising profiles, sell information to advertisers or data brokers, or include developer-operated behavioural tracking.

WeatherAPP may be supported through direct purchases and voluntary donations.

10. Data Sharing, Sale, and Disclosure

WeatherAPP does not sell, rent, or lease user information and does not disclose it to advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing agencies.

WeatherAPP transmits manually entered search terms, selected coordinates, and required request parameters to weather and geocoding providers solely to perform functionality requested by the user.

WeatherAPP does not maintain developer-controlled accounts, analytics profiles, or remote location histories. If we receive a legally valid request, we may respond as required by law. Because preferences and forecast caches are stored locally, we generally do not possess that information.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored data remains until replaced, cleared through Android settings or an available in-app function, or removed when the Application is uninstalled.

Uninstalling normally removes app-specific storage, subject to Android, Google backup, device-manufacturer, and user backup settings outside WeatherAPP's control.

WeatherAPP has no remote account or server-side user profile requiring a separate deletion request.

12. Security

WeatherAPP uses HTTPS when communicating with supported providers. Local information uses standard Android application storage and Android's application sandbox, subject to device and operating-system security.

No electronic storage or network transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Users are responsible for maintaining device, screen-lock, operating-system, and backup security.

13. Android Permissions

WeatherAPP requests only permissions needed for its functionality.

13.1 Internet

The INTERNET permission retrieves forecasts, air-quality information, geocoding results, and update-related information.

13.2 Notifications

On supported Android versions, WeatherAPP may request notification permission when the user enables weather notifications. This permission is optional and can be revoked in Android settings.

13.3 Permissions Not Requested

WeatherAPP does not request Android precise or approximate location, contacts, camera, microphone, or phone-state permissions for normal operation.

Clock and calendar actions use Android system intents after the user chooses the action. The receiving application controls any additional permission or confirmation process.

14. Children's Privacy

WeatherAPP is a general-audience weather application and is not designed to solicit personal information from children.

WeatherAPP does not require an account and does not intentionally collect names, contact information, advertising identifiers, or behavioural profiles from children or other users. The limited processing required for manually requested searches and forecasts applies regardless of age.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us outside normal Application operation, contact us below.

15. International Processing

Third-party providers may process requests on infrastructure outside your province, territory, state, or country. Privacy laws may differ between jurisdictions. Using WeatherAPP involves transmitting the limited request information described in this Policy.

16. Your Choices and Rights

You may use WeatherAPP without Android location permission, choose locations manually, disable notifications, revoke notification permission, clear app cache or storage, uninstall the Application, and decline optional external links, calendar actions, alarm actions, or donation features.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have additional rights. Because WeatherAPP does not maintain user accounts or developer-controlled profiles, we may not possess information associated with a particular user.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to WeatherAPP, providers, legal requirements, or privacy practices. The Last revised date will be updated when material changes are made. Material changes may also be communicated through the Application or Google Play listing.
