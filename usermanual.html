1. Getting Started & Locations

WeatherAPP Libre is built around a strict privacy-first philosophy. The app does not request or use your device's GPS location. Instead, you manually select and manage the locations you care about.

Adding Your First Location

Upon opening the app, tap the location search bar at the top of the screen. Type the name of any city, town, or region. Select your desired location from the search results to load the current weather and astronomical data.

Managing Location Tabs

You can track multiple locations simultaneously using the location tabs situated just below the main menu.

  • Configuring Tab Count & Behavior: Navigate to Drawer > System > Location Tabs to choose between 1 and 4 active tabs. Here you can also toggle whether tabs remain "sticky" at the top of the screen while scrolling, or scroll away with the weather data.
  • Assigning Locations: Tap an empty tab (or use the main search bar) to assign a city to that slot.
  • Switching Locations: Tap any active tab to instantly switch the app's data to that city.
  • Long-Press to Search: Long-press any location tab to instantly open the location search drawer without needing to tap the top search bar.

2. The Main Menu & Navigation

The primary navigation hub is the drop-down menu button located next to the date header. This menu controls which data view is currently active.

Standard Menu Items

  • Current Weather: The main dashboard showing current conditions, hero metrics, and daily summaries.
  • Hourly Forecast: A detailed 36-hour breakdown of temperature, precipitation, and wind.
  • 7-Day Forecast: The daily high/low and condition overview.
  • Natural Light: Solar phases, golden hour, and blue hour tracking.
  • True Dark Sky: Astronomical darkness windows and moon interference.
  • Night Visibility: Ambient light potential based on moon and cloud cover.
  • Solunar Activity: Major and minor wildlife activity periods.
  • Lunar Calendar: Week-by-week lunar phase tracking, eclipses, and atmospheric phenomena.

Menu Organization (Customization)

You have complete control over how this menu looks and behaves. Navigate to Settings > Behaviour > Organize Menu.

  • Reorder: Drag the handle on the left of any item to move it up or down.
  • Hide Items: Toggle the switch on the right to hide an item from the drop-down menu (e.g., if you never use Solunar Activity).
  • Set Default Page: Long-press any row to set it as the default page that loads when you first open the app.
  • Separators: Use the stepper at the top to add visual dividers between groups of tools.

7-Day Forecast Placement

Inside the Organize Menu screen, you will find a toggle for the 7-Day Forecast. You can choose to display it inline (below the Current and Hourly pages) or as a dedicated page inside the drop-down menu.

3. Weather Forecasts & Models

WeatherAPP Libre allows you to choose your preferred meteorological model, giving you transparency into where your data is coming from.

Changing the Forecast Model

Open the main navigation drawer (swipe from the left edge or tap the menu icon) and select your preferred model:

  • GEM: Environment and Climate Change Canada (Best for North America/Canada).
  • GFS / GEFS: NOAA (USA) - Standard and Ensemble models.
  • ECMWF: European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (Highly accurate globally).
  • CMA-GFS: China Meteorological Administration.

Automatic Model Switching: To ensure the highest local accuracy, the app features an automatic model switching capability. Currently, this is exclusively available for locations in China, where the app will automatically switch to the CMA-GFS model (note: this does not include Chinese weather alerts). No other locations currently support automatic model switching. This smart-routing feature is fully toggleable on or off in your settings, and we are actively working to expand this system to automatically route to the most accurate national forecasting infrastructure for other countries and continents.

When you switch models manually, the app will automatically refresh the data for your current location.

Interacting with Forecasts

Tap any day in the 7-Day Forecast list to expand it and view detailed daily metrics and hourly breakdowns for that specific date.

Force Refreshing Data

Weather data is cached locally to save bandwidth and load instantly. If you want to force a fresh pull from the server, simply swipe down from the top of the main weather screen.

4. Natural Light & Astronomy

The app features a suite of advanced astronomical calculators that run entirely on your device.

Natural Light (Golden & Blue Hour)

This page breaks the day into specific solar phases based on the sun's azimuth and elevation. The currently active phase is highlighted with a gold border. The Blue Hour window is calculated from -8° to -4° to provide an extended window for deep cobalt sky photography.

Setting Solar Alarms: Long-press any Natural Light segment (e.g., Golden Hour) to set a customizable alarm for that phase. The default lead time is 10 minutes, which can be adjusted in Drawer > System > Notifications > Solar Phase Alarm Lead Time.

True Dark Sky Window

Designed for astrophotographers, this tool calculates periods of "Astronomical Darkness" (when the sun is below -18°) and cross-references it with the Moon Interference Index. It tells you exactly when the sky will be darkest for deep-sky observation.

Night Visibility Forecast

This tool estimates the ambient light potential for the night. It factors in lunar illumination, moon altitude, and twilight. Crucially, it also integrates with your cached weather data to account for cloud cover attenuation, giving you a realistic idea of how bright the night will actually be.

Solunar Activity

Based on traditional solunar theory, this page calculates Major and Minor activity periods based on lunar transits (when the moon is directly overhead or underfoot) and moonrise/moonset times. Highly useful for fishing and wildlife observation.

Moon Phase & Cycle

View the current lunar illumination, phase category, and synodic cycle progress. Tap the Moon Phase & Cycle button to instantly pop up a detailed calendar view showing the exact dates and times of the next month's major moon phases (New Moon, First Quarter, Full Moon, and Last Quarter).

Lunar Calendar

The Lunar Calendar provides a comprehensive, week-by-week view of lunar phases, rise/set times, and geometric data. Designed for astronomers and photographers, it features a compact inline layout (Sunday through Saturday) with expandable cells for exhaustive daily details.

  • Dual Viewing Scores: Each day displays a Stellar score (favoring dark skies and new moons for deep-space observation) and a Selenic score (favoring illuminated moons for lunar observation).
  • Special Events Filter & Caching: Generate a 3-year forward-looking cache to instantly filter and view rare events like Total Lunar Eclipses (Blood Moons), Supermoons, Micromoons, and Syzygies. Special event days are distinctly highlighted and badged.
  • Navigation: Use the on-screen chevrons, tap the date header to open a date picker, or use your device's hardware volume keys (when enabled in settings) to rapidly cycle through weeks with a built-in performance debounce.
  • Calendar Integration: Long-press any day in the Lunar Calendar to instantly create a reminder in your device's native calendar app for major lunar events.

Lunar Atmospheric Phenomena

WeatherAPP Libre features unique probability heuristics that estimate the likelihood of rare optical events caused by the interaction of moonlight with atmospheric conditions. These probabilities are displayed in the main Moon Phase & Cycle popup.

  • Lunar Moonbow: Estimates the chance of a lunar rainbow based on high illumination, low moon altitude, and specific cloud/precipitation alignments.
  • Lunar Halo & Corona: Calculates the probability of ice crystal or water droplet diffraction rings based on moon brightness and high-altitude/thin cloud cover.
  • Parselene (Mock Moon): Estimates the likelihood of lunar sundogs based on specific cirrus cloud conditions.

Note: If atmospheric conditions are unfavorable, the app will explicitly state that these phenomena are "Unlikely" so you always know the heuristics are actively running.

5. Weather Alerts

WeatherAPP Libre integrates directly with official government alert feeds. Please note that official weather alerts are currently only supported for locations within Canada via the Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC) CAP-CP feed.

  • Zero Synthetic Alerts: The app only displays official, government-issued warnings. It does not generate fake alerts based on forecast models.
  • Alerting Behavior: For supported regions (currently Canada only), the app will automatically monitor and alert you for any active location tab that has a city assigned. You cannot block alerts for individual cities; however, you can dismiss an alert from the banner once it has appeared.
  • Testing Alerts: You can trigger a test ECCC weather alert from the settings menu to ensure your notification permissions and sounds are configured correctly.

6. Settings & Customization

Access the settings drawer to deeply customize the app's behavior and appearance.

Themes and Palettes

Choose from twelve handcrafted themes, including light, dark, and specialized "Boutique" palettes (like Frutiger Aero, Hardy Heron, Terracotta, and Aurora) that dynamically change the color of every card, icon, and chart in the app.

Widget Settings

Navigate to Drawer > System > Widgets to access the centralized widget management menu. Here you can apply Boutique Themes to your home screen widgets (or set them to follow the app/system theme) and use the live-preview Opacity Slider to calibrate widget transparency against your wallpaper.

Behaviour Settings

  • Organize Menu: Customize the drop-down menu layout, visibility, and default launch page (Detailed in Section 2).
  • Units: Toggle between Metric (°C, km/h) and US Customary (°F, mph).
  • Volume Key Date Cycling: Enable this to use your phone's physical volume buttons to rapidly scroll forward and backward through dates while on the Natural Light or Lunar Calendar pages.

Cache Management

Under Settings > Cache, you can adjust the Time-To-Live (TTL) for weather data. The default TTL is aligned to 60 minutes to synchronize with background polling intervals. A shorter TTL means more frequent server requests; a longer TTL saves battery and data but requires manual pull-to-refresh for new updates.

7. Pro-Tips & Hidden Gestures

Master these gestures to navigate WeatherAPP Libre like a professional:

  • Long-Press Location Tab: Opens the location search drawer instantly.
  • Long-Press Menu Item (in Settings): Sets that page as your default launch screen.
  • Long-Press Natural Light Phase: Instantly sets a solar alarm for that specific time of day.
  • Long-Press Lunar Calendar Day: Instantly creates a native calendar reminder for a major moon phase or eclipse.
  • Pull-Down to Refresh: Swiping down on the main screen forces a cache bypass and fetches fresh data.
  • Volume Keys (When Enabled): Scrub through future and past dates on the Natural Light page, or cycle through weeks on the Lunar Calendar, without touching the screen.
  • Tap 7-Day Forecast Lines: Expand any day in the weekly forecast to see granular daily details.
  • Tap Moon Phase & Cycle: Instantly opens a popup displaying the next month of upcoming lunar phases, events, and atmospheric heuristics.
